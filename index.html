About DJ Moe d finest

More than a DJ. More than a producer. This is the sound, energy and journey of DJ Moe d finest.

DJ Moe d finest is a DJ, producer and recording artist driven by a genuine passion for music, creativity and unforgettable moments. His sound brings together different influences to create an energetic musical experience that connects with people.

From the atmosphere of a live party to the headphones of someone discovering a new record, DJ Moe d finest believes that music should do more than simply play — it should create a feeling.

His musical world moves through Afrobeat, Afropop, Amapiano and Hip-Hop , combining rhythm, energy and personality while keeping the focus on originality and entertainment.

Whether behind the decks, inside the studio or working on a new release, DJ Moe d finest continues to build his own sound and identity. Every set, every production and every recording is another step in the journey.
